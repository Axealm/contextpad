# Personas

## Primary Persona: Busy Business Operator

Works across clients, vendors, and internal teams. Receives many emails that contain meetings, requests, deadlines, and preparation tasks.

Needs:

- Capture context quickly
- Avoid missing deadlines
- Keep email and notes connected
- Search later by person, company, meeting, or task

Pain points:

- Too many tools
- Hard to remember why a meeting exists
- Tasks are hidden in long email threads

## Secondary Persona: Junior Engineer Or Analyst

Uses the app to prepare for meetings, organize technical research, and track action items from email.

Needs:

- Structured task breakdown
- Clear deadline extraction
- Source email traceability
- A portfolio-worthy implementation they can explain

Pain points:

- Ambiguous instructions
- Overhead of formal task tools
- Difficulty keeping decision history

## Reviewer Persona: Hiring Engineer

Reviews the portfolio.

Looks for:

- Clear product thinking
- Realistic architecture
- Security awareness
- Tests and operations thinking
- Evidence of AI-assisted development with human judgment
